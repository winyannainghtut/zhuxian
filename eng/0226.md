# Chapter 226 - Intention

A cough echoed from the side. It was not particularly loud, but it sounded labored and ragged. Lu Xueqi and the others turned toward the sound and saw Tian Buyi sitting upon the stone steps, a trace of agony etched upon his face as he coughed relentlessly.

Lu Xueqi’s brows knitted slightly, a flicker of concern surfacing in her eyes. Given Tian Buyi’s profound cultivation, he had long surpassed the stage of being susceptible to any common ailment, let alone a slight cough. Clearly, his internal organs and meridians had suffered severe trauma.

As Lu Xueqi hesitated in silence, Xiao Huan quietly stepped forward.

Tian Buyi looked slightly surprised as he lifted his head. Xiao Huan smiled gently and said, "Senior... thank you for saving me, my grandfather, and the Taoist a few days ago."

A faint smile appeared on Tian Buyi’s weary face. He replied calmly, "A trivial matter, not worth mentioning. This is an ill-omened place, not somewhere you should linger. If you have nothing else to attend to, you had best leave at once."

Zhou Yixian nodded repeatedly. "Yes, yes, what he says is absolutely right! Xiao Huan, let us make haste and leave!"

Xiao Huan rolled her eyes at her grandfather, then said to Tian Buyi, "Senior, is your body alright? Is there anything I can do to help?"

Tian Buyi shook his head. "I am in no great danger. You two should simply leave. Otherwise, if that person returns, I fear you will not be fortunate enough to escape a second time."

As he spoke, a faint shade of black suddenly flickered across his stout face. Pain flashed in his eyes once more, and he could not help but break into another coughing fit, his voice growing noticeably rasper.

Standing alone to the side, the Shaman Demon kept his gaze fixed upon Tian Buyi, observing his every movement. Behind his black veil, only his eyes revealed a look of deep contemplation.

Unable to resist Zhou Yixian's relentless urging from behind, and realizing she truly could be of no assistance here, Xiao Huan bowed her farewell to Tian Buyi. She then followed Zhou Yixian and the Wild Dog Taoist toward the exit.

Yet after taking only a few steps, she could not resist glancing back at the Shaman Demon standing off to the side. As if discovering something startling, she paused and whispered to Zhou Yixian, "Grandfather, look at that person. Why is his attire identical to my... that master of mine?"

Zhou Yixian had not caught on at first, asking in bewilderment, "What master... er!" He stopped short, squinting his eyes as he examined the Shaman Demon. After a moment's contemplation, he said, "There are far too many strange eccentrics in this world. It's hardly surprising that a few might dress like that ghostly master of yours. Pay him no mind, let us hurry on!"

Xiao Huan acknowledged him and continued walking, yet she could not resist looking back at the Shaman Demon once more. She saw that the masked figure paid no heed to the three of them, standing silently in place with his eyes fixed on Tian Buyi.

Soon, Zhou Yixian, Xiao Huan, and the Wild Dog Taoist left the derelict morgue. The desolate clearing suddenly became even quieter. Tian Buyi and Lu Xueqi turned their attention toward the mysterious man in black.

Tian Buyi asked flatly, "Does Your Excellency still have business here?"

The Shaman Demon remained silent for a moment. His gaze shifted from Tian Buyi to Lu Xueqi, before returning to Tian Buyi. He seemed on the verge of speaking, but ultimately held his tongue. His body drifted backward, quietly melting into the deep gloom of the night.

A chilly night wind blew in from afar. The entire abandoned morgue fell into absolute silence. Not even the usual chirping of insects in the wilderness could be heard; all was dead quiet.

For some reason, a wave of unease swept through Lu Xueqi’s heart.

Tian Buyi raised his head and gazed at the sky for a long time, as though contemplating something profound.

Lu Xueqi did not know why he had suddenly spaced out and dared not disturb him. Yet after a long while, seeing no movement from him and growing worried about his injuries, she was just about to speak when Tian Buyi abruptly lowered his head, followed by a violent coughing fit far worse than before.

Lu Xueqi was startled. Not knowing what to do, she could only ask, "Uncle-Master Tian, are you alright?"

Tian Buyi coughed for a long time before finally steadying himself, having caught his breath. He slowly waved his hand, indicating he was fine.

Lu Xueqi still could not help saying, "Uncle-Master Tian, this place is not far from our Mount Qing Yun. I believe we should return to Qing Yun first. After meeting with the senior elders, we can plan our next move carefully!"

Hearing her words, Tian Buyi frowned as if suddenly remembering something. He turned to look at Lu Xueqi and asked, "During the time I was away, how are things on Big Bamboo Peak? And your Aunt Su Ru... is she well?"

Lu Xueqi nodded. "They are all well. It is just that no one knew of Uncle-Master’s whereabouts, so everyone has been deeply anxious."

Tian Buyi smiled faintly, as though a heavy boulder had been lifted from his chest, though a hint of bitterness lingered in that smile.

Taking note of his expression, Lu Xueqi hesitated before testing the waters: "Uncle-Master Tian, those people mentioned earlier that you had been fighting against a devil. Who was that devil?"

Tian Buyi looked at her, his brows furrowing in silence.

Meeting his gaze, Lu Xueqi suddenly noticed that beneath the exhaustion on her Uncle-Master’s face, a faint, unnatural wisp of black mist seemed to flicker intermittently across his skin.

Could his meridians have been injured by the eerie demonic art that bound him? Lu Xueqi was secretly worried, yet another far more pressing matter compelled her to speak.

Hesitating for a fleeting moment, she finally asked in a low, clear voice, "Could that person... be Sect Master Uncle Daoxuan?"

Tian Buyi’s body shook violently. An imposing, terrifying light shot from his eyes as he demanded coldly, "What did you say?"

Lu Xueqi quickly explained, "Before this disciple descended the mountain, Master placed great trust in me and revealed a secret from the Founders Ancestral Hall involving herself, Uncle-Master Tian, and Aunt Su."

Tian Buyi froze, shock written across his face. Gradually, his expression softened. After a long while, he let out a profound sigh. "To think Shuiyue actually told you."

Lu Xueqi said, "Because Sect Master Uncle Daoxuan and Uncle-Master Tian disappeared simultaneously, Mount Qing Yun fell into turmoil. Master was deeply worried that Sect Master Uncle had fallen prey to an inner demon. Yet Senior Brother Xiao Yicai of the Long Gate clearly knew nothing of the matter. Thus, Master took the initiative to oversee Mount Qing Yun and commanded this disciple to descend and search for you both."

Tian Buyi was silent for a moment before asking, "If you found the Sect Master down the mountain, and if he truly ended up as your master feared... did Shuiyue tell you what you must do?"

Lu Xueqi’s face turned slightly pale, as if this secret were an immense burden to bear. Under Tian Buyi’s gaze, she took a deep breath and said resolutely, "Before descending the mountain, this disciple went with Master to the Founders Ancestral Hall on Tongtian Peak. Before the spirit tablets of the past patriarchs of the Qing Yun Sect, I swore a solemn oath: if the situation cannot be saved, for the sake of Qing Yun’s millennial honor, this disciple shall fight unto death without mercy, and shall never reveal a single shred of this secret for the rest of my life."

Tian Buyi gazed deeply at Lu Xueqi, then slowly nodded, letting out a long sigh. "Though I have never liked Shuiyue’s temperament, I must admit she has raised a remarkable disciple."

Lu Xueqi kept her head lowered, replying, "Uncle-Master Tian overpraises me."

Tian Buyi said softly, "Among the second-generation disciples of Qing Yun, there are well over a thousand. Yet looking across them all, how many could shoulder such a monumental burden? Sigh..." He paused, then continued, "Your guess earlier was correct. The person I fought with here a few days ago was indeed your Sect Master Uncle Daoxuan."

Though Lu Xueqi had long suspected as much, hearing it confirmed by Tian Buyi still made her tremble. After a long pause, she asked in a low voice, "Then... how is Sect Master Uncle’s condition...?"

Tian Buyi snorted and shook his head with a sigh. "He has sunk far too deep. He can no longer pull himself out."

Lu Xueqi fell silent.

Tian Buyi continued, "The intricacies of this matter are a long story, but since you already know the origin, I have no reason to hide anything from you. That day, I noticed signs that Senior Brother Daoxuan had deviated into demonic corruption, so I went up Tongtian Peak. When I reached the Founders Ancestral Hall, I found that he truly had... We ended up fighting in the ancestral hall. Though he had succumbed to demons, his cultivation had not diminished in the slightest. In the end, after a fierce struggle, I was subdued by him."

Listening to his tale, Lu Xueqi was privately stunned. She was well acquainted with the immense cultivation of both Tian Buyi and Reverend Daoxuan. The intensity of a clash between two of Qing Yun’s greatest titans at the back of Tongtian Peak was easy to imagine. Though Tian Buyi spoke of it casually now, the scene back then must have been terrifying.

A bitter smile appeared on Tian Buyi’s face. "When I went there that day, I was prepared for the worst. If I could awaken Senior Brother Daoxuan, that would have been ideal; if not, I would do my utmost to fight. Back when your master Shuiyue and I eavesdropped on that secret in the ancestral hall, we heard Senior Brother Wan mention that when someone falls into demonic corruption, the invasion of demonic power damages their essence and drastically diminishes their cultivation. I knew Senior Brother Daoxuan’s cultivation was deeper than mine, so my intention was merely to drag him down with me if necessary. After all, this secret could never be allowed to spread."

A profound sense of reverence rose within Lu Xueqi, and she said sincerely, "Uncle-Master Tian’s devotion can be witnessed by the sun and moon. The ancestors will surely bless you."

Tian Buyi shook his head. "Who could have anticipated that when Senior Brother Daoxuan and I crossed hands, his cultivation was as boundlessly profound as ever despite his corruption? After several fierce clashes, I was defeated and captured. Yet for some unknown reason, he showed no intent to kill me. Instead, he brought me down the mountain in secret, came to this ghastly, abandoned morgue, and imprisoned me here."

Reminded by his words, Lu Xueqi asked hurriedly, "Then were you injured, Uncle-Master Tian? Your complexion looks very poor."

Tian Buyi froze, seemingly not understanding her implication at first, but then chuckled self-deprecatingly. "Anyone shoved into a coffin and locked away for days on end would hardly have a blooming complexion."

Lu Xueqi frowned, a lingering unease tugging at her heart, yet unable to pinpoint what was amiss, she remained quiet.

Tian Buyi glanced at her. "You know the broad outline now. What do you plan to do?"

Lu Xueqi’s brows locked together. "Uncle-Master Tian, where... where is Sect Master Uncle Daoxuan now?"

Tian Buyi shook his head. "Ever since he succumbed to demonic corruption, his actions have become utterly unpredictable. He often leaves us behind and returns only after several days. By my count, he left this morgue just yesterday, so he likely won't return for several more days. Yet that is not certain; occasionally, he returns the very next day."

Lu Xueqi hesitated, then said, "Uncle-Master Tian, perhaps we should return to the mountain first. Although this matter must not be publicized, if we consult my master and Aunt Su Ru, the three of you senior elders can surely devise a better plan together."

Tian Buyi was silent for a moment before ultimately shaking his head. "That will not do. For one, Senior Brother Daoxuan's mind has drastically warped; no one can predict what horrors he might commit. Secondly, if we return to the mountain and lose his trail completely, what then?"

He paused, then added, "How about this: you return to the mountain first and inform Shuiyue and your Aunt Su Ru of what transpired, bidding them to come swiftly."

Lu Xueqi hesitated. "What if Sect Master Uncle Daoxuan returns tonight?"

Tian Buyi smiled faintly. Without answering immediately, he slowly rose to his feet.

He was short and stout, his appearance far from distinguished. Yet as he stood there casually against the wind, a natural aura of imposing majesty radiated from him, commanding absolute reverence.

"A lifetime of cultivation—for what purpose?" Tian Buyi murmured softly. "How can a man of valor cower before the battlefield?"

Lu Xueqi stood up quietly behind him. In the past, in her eyes, Tian Buyi had been nothing more than the mentor of *that person*, hardly worth her personal attention. But at this moment, she felt a profound, heartfelt admiration for this senior uncle.

Gritting her teeth, she said in a clear, resonant voice, "Uncle-Master Tian, you have just escaped confinement and need quiet rest to recover your vitality. Tonight, I shall stand guard for you. At dawn tomorrow, I will rush back to Qing Yun and inform Master and Aunt Su Ru to descend. If... if Sect Master Uncle truly returns tonight..."

Tian Buyi looked slightly surprised, glancing at her. "What then?"

Lu Xueqi smiled faintly, her beauty radiating purity in the chill night wind. "Among the disciples of Qing Yun, Uncle-Master Tian is not the only one who can look upon death as a homecoming!"

Tian Buyi stared at Lu Xueqi for a long time, then clapped his hands and laughed. "Well said! Splendidly said! What a phrase—'look upon death as a homecoming'!"

Lu Xueqi smiled gently. "Uncle-Master Tian, please sit and regulate your breathing at once."

Tian Buyi said no more, simply nodding, and reseated himself on the mossy stone steps, closing his eyes. Lu Xueqi looked around the clearing; the night was cold and bleak, devoid of any human presence or even a shadow.

The night was deep. Who could predict what tomorrow would bring?

Refusing to dwell on it, she sat down below Tian Buyi and closed her eyes.

She did not know how long she sat there. Her mind gradually turned crystal clear. Though her eyes were shut, the circulation of spiritual energy within her body allowed her to perceive every blade of grass and tree near and far as clearly as if she were seeing them with her own eyes.

She felt a wave of comfort. In recent years, she had frequently wandered the world, tasting the bitter torment of lovesickness, yet her cultivation had continued to advance without stagnating. Yet she soon realized that while her spiritual perception was razor-sharp, it seemed completely ineffective against Tian Buyi sitting right beside her; she could not even detect his heartbeat.

Lu Xueqi felt another surge of admiration for him. It seemed these senior elders of Qing Yun truly possessed staggering cultivation.

As these thoughts drifted through her mind, Tian Buyi's voice suddenly reached her ear: "Niece Lu..."

Lu Xueqi opened her eyes and said quickly, "Uncle-Master Tian, you may simply call me Xueqi."

Tian Buyi looked at her, a profound glint flashing in his eyes. He nodded slowly. "Xueqi."

Lu Xueqi smiled gently. "Yes, Uncle-Master Tian. Is there something on your mind?"

Tian Buyi averted his gaze, falling silent for a moment. Lu Xueqi found it slightly strange as his eyes seemed to drift toward some unknown point in the distance. After a long pause, he suddenly asked:

"In the past, you were acquainted with that good-for-nothing seventh disciple of mine, weren't you?"

Lu Xueqi was startled. For a moment, even someone as perpetually calm as her felt entirely flustered. Beneath her fair, jade-like cheeks, two faint patches of pink rose inexplicably.

Forcing her racing heart to calm down, Lu Xueqi managed to compose herself, though her expression still carried a trace of embarrassment and shyness. She asked softly, "Yes... Uncle-Master Tian, why... why do you suddenly bring this up?"

Tian Buyi remained expressionless, neither pleased nor angry, as though discussing something utterly trivial. He said calmly, "I heard that over these years, while traveling down the mountain, your bond with Old Seventh was far from shallow. Because of him, your master punished you multiple times. On one occasion, in broad daylight, before Senior Brother Daoxuan, your master, and all the elders, you even personally rejected a marriage proposal made by the Valley Master of Fenxiang Valley himself?"

Lu Xueqi was completely at a loss as to why Tian Buyi was suddenly asking these questions. Her face burned intensely, flushed crimson. Her thoughts were in utter disarray. To speak of such intimate matters before this usually distant Uncle-Master made her heart race far more than it ever had before her respected master Shuiyue.

"...Yes," Lu Xueqi stammered for the very first time. After a long pause, she whispered, "However, my rejection of the proposal was not entirely for his sake. It was simply that I did not wish it, so..."

Tian Buyi abruptly cut her off and asked directly:

"Do you like my Old Seventh?"

A buzzing sound erupted in Lu Xueqi’s mind. Her face felt as hot as fire. She looked at Tian Buyi and saw his bright, piercing eyes staring straight at her.

Under that piercing gaze, Lu Xueqi suddenly found an inexplicable surge of courage from deep within her soul. She sat upright, took a deep breath, looked straight into Tian Buyi’s eyes, and answered loudly, crisply, and without hesitation:

"Yes!"

The word rang out like ice snapping and snow cracking, clear and melodic, without the slightest doubt or vacillation—just like the pristine, immaculate light in her eyes, untainted by any impurity.

The corners of Tian Buyi’s mouth twitched, and then he burst into uproarious laughter! He laughed without restraint, filled with genuine, heartfelt joy.

Hearing his laughter, Lu Xueqi felt bashful, yet unconsciously, her impression of this stout Uncle-Master grew infinitely warmer and closer.

As Tian Buyi’s laughter gradually subsided and he looked back at her, Lu Xueqi smiled faintly, though an inexplicable sorrow soon crept into her heart. She whispered, "Unfortunately, as he is now... I fear the elders and masters can no longer tolerate him. If only he could return to Qing Yun, how wonderful that would be!"

Tian Buyi rolled his eyes and snorted coldly. "What do you mean 'return'? I have never once declared that I expelled that good-for-nothing fellow from my sect!"

Lu Xueqi froze, not understanding his meaning, and looked up at him.

Tian Buyi said calmly, "I know what you are thinking. Aren't you simply worried about your master?"

Lu Xueqi lowered her head. After a while, she said, "Master only has my best interests at heart, and she did nothing wrong. The fault is mine. I understand."

Tian Buyi suddenly spat loudly. This somewhat crude gesture startled Lu Xueqi, making her glance up at him.

Tian Buyi rolled his eyes in the direction of distant Mount Qing Yun and said, "In my opinion, that master of yours is acting more and more like your grandmaster Zhenchu back in the day. She can't sort out her own affairs, yet she insists on meddling in everything—especially poking her nose into her disciples' hearts! Could it be that like her master, she has gone senile?"

Lu Xueqi scolded softly, "Uncle-Master Tian, how can you speak so recklessly?"

Tian Buyi glanced at her, chuckled, and waved his broad hand. "Fret not. Once this ordeal is concluded, your matter shall be entirely upon my shoulders."

Lu Xueqi froze. "What?"

Tian Buyi scoffed, "Speaking of which, you aren't the only one. Don't you have a senior sister named Wenmin?"

Lu Xueqi nodded. "Yes. Senior Sister Wenmin... she and Senior Brother Song Daren of Big Bamboo Peak have always had feelings for each other." At this point, a smile naturally curved her lips.

Tian Buyi glared at her and grumbled, "Song Daren is a wooden blockhead."

Lu Xueqi couldn't help letting out a giggle. "When Senior Sister Wenmin talks about Senior Brother Song in private, she says the exact same thing."

Tian Buyi shook his head, seemingly exasperated by his overly honest eldest disciple, perhaps feeling Song Daren had embarrassed him. Then he said, "Rest assured. In the future, I shall personally ascend Little Bamboo Peak to propose marriage to your master for both of those good-for-nothing rascals under my tutelage."

The moment those words were spoken, Lu Xueqi's face flushed beet-red—a reaction never before seen on her. In her fluster, she could only protest shyly, "Uncle-Master Tian, if you keep teasing this disciple like this, I... I shall truly get angry."

Tian Buyi looked at her. "I speak the honest truth. When have I ever teased you? Could it be you are unwilling to marry my Old Seventh?"

Lu Xueqi said frantically, "No... ah, that's not what I meant! I mean..."

On Tian Buyi’s round face, his eyes blinked innocently as he asked with utmost solemnity, "Then what do you mean?"

Lu Xueqi was rendered speechless, her words caught in her throat. Whether from anxiety or bashfulness, a radiant pink glow suffused her porcelain skin, making her breathtakingly gorgeous.

Tian Buyi smiled gently. "Very well, I shall say no more. You are an exceptionally clever young woman. What I mean, you surely understand?"

Lu Xueqi drew a deep breath and gradually composed herself, though the rosy blush on her lovely face still lingered like rouge. Yet her gaze was once again as clear and bright as before. After a moment, she nodded slowly and firmly. "Yes, this disciple understands."

Tian Buyi stared at her, pressing the question: "Are you willing?"

The blush on Lu Xueqi’s cheeks seemed to deepen, yet this time, she was completely calm and poised, answering just as she had before:

"Yes!"
