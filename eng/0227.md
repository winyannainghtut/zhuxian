# Chapter 227 - Reunion

Tian Buyi let out a long breath, a broad smile spreading across his face.

However, Lu Xueqi was meticulous by nature. Pondering for a moment, she looked at Tian Buyi and asked, "Uncle-Master Tian, your meaning just now... is that he can return to Qing Yun?"

Tian Buyi snorted. "Ten years ago, the truth came out on Mount Qing Yun, and Old Seventh departed. Afterward, I reflected upon it repeatedly and felt that Old Seventh had done nothing wrong in that entire affair. Through bizarre twists of fate and sheer absurdity, he somehow turned against Qing Yun. In my entire life, I have taken in only seven disciples. Though each is good-for-nothing, if anyone expects me to casually pretend none of it happened, to act as if I never took in this disciple and wash my hands of him, that is utterly impossible."

Lu Xueqi felt like someone who had spent ages in the dark, suddenly glimpsing a faint beam of light ahead. She was both astonished and overjoyed.

Tian Buyi continued, "I know that if we truly pursue this, there will be countless obstacles. But over the past ten years, I have continually kept an eye on Old Seventh. Fortunately, his conscience has remained intact, and I have never heard of him committing any heinous atrocities."

Lu Xueqi chimed in hastily, "Indeed, I have also kept watch over him, and I have never heard of him performing any evil deeds since joining the Demonic Sect..."

As she spoke, she noticed Tian Buyi watching her with a half-smile. Her face burned hot, and her voice trailed off into a whisper.

Tian Buyi nodded. "You have certainly been devoted. Over these ten years, I imagine you have not had an easy time either."

Lu Xueqi was silent.

Tian Buyi coughed a few times, seeming slightly short of breath, before saying, "In short, as long as he still acknowledges me as his master, his affairs—no," he looked at Lu Xueqi and smiled gently, "both of your affairs, I will certainly never ignore."

Lu Xueqi gently bit her lower lip. After a moment, she lowered her head slightly and murmured, "This disciple thanks Uncle-Master."

Tian Buyi nodded, but could not help coughing again. It seemed that heartfelt conversation had exhausted him despite his joy, and the faint black mist on his face appeared to darken slightly.

Lu Xueqi could not help feeling concerned. "Uncle-Master Tian, you should refrain from speaking too much for now. Please rest and recuperate first."

As she spoke, she looked up at the sky and added, "Judging by the sky, dawn will arrive in at most two hours. When it breaks, I will rush back to Mount Qing Yun to inform Master and Aunt Su Ru."

Tian Buyi nodded and closed his eyes once more.

Lu Xueqi took a deep breath, calming the lingering restlessness in her heart, and shut her eyes as well. Yet at the same time, a faint smile quietly blossomed at the corners of her lips.

A small, serene smile of happiness that belonged to her alone!

In the distant night sky, a faint star peeked out from behind the heavy clouds like the pure eyes of a young maiden. Moments later, a breeze blew past, and a dark cloud drifted over, cloaking it once more.

Beneath the clouds, two streaks of light faintly cut across the sky, heading toward this very location.

***

Zhou Yixian led Xiao Huan and the Wild Dog Taoist in a hasty retreat from the derelict morgue, which had brought them nothing but terrible misfortune.

Though Zhou Yixian was advanced in years, his footsteps were faster than those of young men, leaving Xiao Huan and the Wild Dog Taoist struggling to keep pace.

After walking for a long while, the broad avenue ahead finally became clear. Xiao Huan, panting slightly, grumbled, "Grandfather, aren't you tired? Why are you walking so fast?"

Zhou Yixian looked at the road ahead, then glanced back toward the morgue, which was no longer visible. Only then did he exhale in relief and stop. "What do you understand? We are truly blessed by great fortune to have escaped with our lives! If we don't know what's good for us and stay as far away from that morgue as possible, wouldn't we be asking for disaster?"

Xiao Huan paused, recalling their harrowing experiences in the morgue over the past few days, especially that mysterious fiend. A lingering fear seized her heart, and she shook her head. "To think that right at the foot of Mount Qing Yun, there would be such a terrifying demonic fiend."

Zhou Yixian suddenly sneered coldly. "If it weren't at the foot of Mount Qing Yun, you wouldn't have seen him at all!"

Xiao Huan and the Wild Dog Taoist both froze. "What?"

Zhou Yixian frowned, realizing he had misspoken. Looking left and right, he waved his hand dismissively. "Enough, let us leave quickly. Once we enter the city where crowds gather, there will be nothing to fear."

Xiao Huan looked at him strangely, but Zhou Yixian ignored her and took the lead. She hurried a few steps to catch up, intending to press the matter, but Zhou Yixian coughed, looked up at the sky, and said, "Xiao Huan, look, the night sky is quite pleasant tonight..."

Xiao Huan spat in disgust. "On a night with dark winds and gloomy chills, you actually dare call it pleasant?"

Zhou Yixian laughed dryly and quickened his pace. Xiao Huan watched her grandfather’s back with deep suspicion, sensing that he was hiding something.

As the three walked along, a gust of wind suddenly whistled past behind them. A dark shadow was rapidly pursuing them from the direction of the morgue.

All three were startled, the same terrifying thought flashing in their minds: *Has that devil caught up to us so quickly?*

Turning their heads, they were stunned. The newcomer was dressed in plain black, but it was not the mysterious fiend cloaked in black mist; rather, it was the Shaman Demon they had met shortly before.

The Shaman Demon naturally saw the trio as well, but paid them no mind. He did not even slow down as he swept past, clearly eager to leave the area as soon as possible.

Just as the Shaman Demon was about to flash past, a female voice called out from behind: "Wait!"

The Shaman Demon paused, swiveling in midair, and halted his steps. Turning back, he saw the two men looking astonished at the young girl between them, while the girl seemed momentarily dazed, unable to speak.

The Shaman Demon sized Xiao Huan up. "Young lady, did you call me?"

Xiao Huan hesitated. "Yes."

The Shaman Demon asked, "What is it?"

Xiao Huan faltered. She had called out to this masked man purely on instinct, feeling his attire was identical to that of her mysterious master whom she had met only twice. Were it not for the completely different auras between them—the Shaman Demon lacked the unique, chilling ghostly aura of Mr. Ghost—she would have almost blurted out "Master."

With the Shaman Demon questioning her, she was momentarily at a loss. After an awkward pause, she asked, "Um... may I ask, sir, do you happen to know another person dressed exactly like you?"

The Shaman Demon froze, then shook his head. "I do not."

Xiao Huan frowned and wanted to say more, but Zhou Yixian yanked her behind him. Smiling at the masked figure, he said, "Honored sir, young girls know nothing of manners. Please do not take it to heart."

With that, he glared fiercely at Xiao Huan and dragged her away. The Wild Dog Taoist hurriedly followed. Xiao Huan felt embarrassed, but still muttered, "Grandfather, what are you doing?"

Zhou Yixian snorted. "Are you looking for trouble?"

Xiao Huan lowered her voice, peeking back at the Shaman Demon, who was still standing there. She whispered to Zhou Yixian, "But they really look too much alike..."

Zhou Yixian couldn't be bothered to answer, gripping her tighter as he marched toward the distant road. Watching the three depart until faint murmurs from Xiao Huan were carried away by the wind, the Shaman Demon felt utterly bewildered. After a moment, he let out a bitter laugh, shook his head, and prepared to leave.

Just as his body moved, he whipped around, facing back toward the derelict morgue. Sharp light flashed in his eyes as he stared intently into the distance.

Along his line of sight, in the far sky, two silhouettes could be faintly seen chasing each other, diving straight toward the morgue.

The Shaman Demon hesitated for a moment, but ultimately discarded his curiosity. Shaking his head, he drifted away into the night.

His instincts warned him that the morgue was no place of fortune—better to stay far away!

***

Back within the abandoned morgue, silence still reigned. Lu Xueqi slowly opened her eyes, her gaze sweeping across the surroundings. All was quiet without any anomalies, and her heart settled.

Tian Buyi remained seated on the stone steps, eyes closed in meditation. From time to time, the night wind blew past, yet for some reason, his robes never fluttered, as though the wind deliberately detoured around him.

Lu Xueqi felt a stir in her heart. Sensing something peculiar, her brows knit slightly as her gaze fell upon Tian Buyi’s body. The longer she looked, the deeper her confusion grew.

From the moment she had rescued Tian Buyi from that restrictive coffin, she had noticed that this senior uncle of Big Bamboo Peak appeared far stouter than in her memory. Yet his face had not widened; only his torso seemed two sizes broader, causing his robes to appear taut and strained.

Though Tian Buyi had always been stout, Lu Xueqi felt this particular bulkiness was profoundly wrong. Yet unable to discern what was amiss, she felt a wave of worry. Although she had shattered the coffin's ward with her Heavenly Tome cultivation, she had been thoroughly shocked by the bizarre demonic power binding him—it was unlike anything she had ever encountered.

Determined to ask Tian Buyi clearly, she turned to speak. But before words left her lips, Tian Buyi, who had been resting with eyes shut, abruptly opened them. A piercing light shot from his gaze, not toward Lu Xueqi, but locked with a contorted expression onto the pitch-black sky.

Lu Xueqi’s heart tightened. She stood up and followed his gaze. A moment later, her body shook violently.

From the ink-black firmament, a dark shadow plunged downward like lightning, enveloped in a swirling vortex of black mist. Even before it touched the ground, an overwhelming wave of demonic energy surged forth like an angry tide.

Tian Buyi slowly rose to his feet.

Lu Xueqi felt her throat go dry, asking in a low whisper, "Is it him?"

Tian Buyi nodded slowly, his voice deep. "It is him."

Lu Xueqi never took her eyes off that dark shadow. Letting out a soft sigh, she spoke no further. Only the Tianya Sword in her hand began to glow, its azure radiance gently unfurling.

*Whoosh!*

With a howl of wind, the dark figure descended from the sky, landing in the courtyard of the morgue. Spotting Tian Buyi and Lu Xueqi standing by the ruined entrance, the figure seemed momentarily taken aback, yet showed no hint of fear. Moments later, a series of bizarre, rasping cackles broke the silence.

Lu Xueqi looked closely. The newcomer was entirely shrouded in churning, thick black mist, obscuring his face and form. And from that laugh alone, she could never associate it with the venerable, benevolent Sect Master of her memory.

The mysterious figure measured Tian Buyi and Lu Xueqi, his gaze finally lingering on the Tianya Sword in her hand. He spoke rasply, "She was the one who rescued you?"

Tian Buyi gave a cold snort and did not answer. He stared at the black mist enveloping the figure, his eyes burning with fury as he took a step forward.

Lu Xueqi remained expressionless, but took two steps in a flanking direction. In an instant, she and Tian Buyi formed a pincer stance against the mysterious figure.

Tian Buyi's cultivation went without saying, and Lu Xueqi's current prowess was enough to boast across the world. Yet that mysterious figure seemed utterly arrogant, paying them no heed whatsoever. Instead, he burst into deep, grating laughter that echoed through the desolate ruins like the wailing of ghosts.

"Tian Buyi, do you still dare to cross hands with me?"

Tian Buyi said grimly, "You have sunk far into demonic corruption. I have no choice but to fight."

The man sneered. "You say I have fallen into corruption, but how do you know it is not you who fails to see the truth?"

Tian Buyi raised his right hand. Light flared, and his magical treasure, the Caihong Sword, appeared in his grip, burning like blazing fire. Word by word, he declared, "This 'Xuan Yin Ghost Qi' of yours has claimed countless innocent lives and wretched souls! What more do you have to say?"

When the words "Xuan Yin Ghost Qi" reached Lu Xueqi's ears, her expression changed drastically. As far as she knew, this sinister technique was no demonic sect art, but an ancient Ghost Dao sorcery thought to be long lost, entirely reliant on harvesting the vitality of living humans and the dark energy of wandering ghosts. Its cruelty was unimaginable.

Why would such grotesque sorcery appear upon this man before them? It was utterly unfathomable!

The black mist around the mysterious figure churned, emitting several cold chuckles. Just as he was about to speak, he stopped, turning slightly to look toward the sky behind him.

Tian Buyi and Lu Xueqi sensed it as well, and their faces shifted.

From midair, a figure descended like thunder! Before the person arrived, a howling gale swept over, causing the smaller rocks on the ground to slowly roll. With such immense momentum, the newcomer's cultivation was evident.

Tonight, this abandoned morgue had become a gathering storm of legendary figures arriving one after another.

Yet life was often shaped by twists of fate following coincidences.

*Swoosh!*

Unlike the mysterious figure, though this newcomer arrived with thunderous fury, his landing was as light as a feather. Only a sharp, piercing whistle tore through the silence as he landed, spun half a circle, and came to a firm halt without stirring the dust. He turned his head to survey the scene.

A heartbeat later, he froze.

Lu Xueqi froze.

Tian Buyi also froze.

As though boiling blood had suddenly ignited in his chest, Ghost Li's body trembled uncontrollably. Less than ten paces in front of him stood a short, stout man. Though his complexion looked grey and weary, and his frame strangely bloated, Ghost Li recognized him at the very first glance...

This was the man who had raised him from childhood, who had passed down cultivation and principles, the master he had revered and feared the most his entire life!

His mouth opened slightly, yet no sound came out. Ten years. It felt as though ten thousand words churned in his chest, yet in this fleeting moment, all dissolved into silence.

Tian Buyi stared deeply at Ghost Li. No, who cared about that damn Ghost Li? The person he saw was merely the good-for-nothing seventh disciple under his seat—Zhang Xiaofan.

Ten years of separation. The youth of the past was no longer young; faint traces of white even dusted his temples. Over these ten years, he must have suffered terribly! Tian Buyi had never stopped imagining meeting this disciple who had turned his back on Qing Yun. He had envisioned giving him a furious scolding before lecturing him earnestly, hoping he would turn back.

Yet at this moment, perhaps he was truly growing old. Not a single word of reprimand came to mind. His trembling lips finally curved into a gentle smile, and he called out softly:

"Old Seventh!"

In Ghost Li's mind, a thunderous roar erupted. These two simple words shattered every wall he had erected around his heart over the past decade. Everything from the past flooded back in vivid detail—the verdant, beautiful Big Bamboo Peak, the rustling waves of the bamboo sea whispering in the wind.

He stood dazed, trembling from head to toe. Even the monkey Xiao Hui crouching upon his shoulder remained uncharacteristically silent.

A scorching sensation burned in his throat, yet beneath it was a warmth he had not felt in years. Once upon a time, that had been his most precious memory.

And now, that person stood right there, calling out to him.

"Master!"

In that instant, he seemed to return to that reckless, ordinary boy of the past. Driven by the fire burning in his chest, he cried out with all his soul.

At the corners of his eyes, tears quietly gleamed.
