# Chapter 229 - Parting

"Ah..."

Like a sound dragged painfully from the depths of his throat, raspy and sluggish, Tian Buyi let out a faint gasp. His frame began to tremble, but quickly stilled. Only upon his face, the once faint, elusive black mist now churned so densely that it seemed on the verge of swallowing his entire visage.

Lu Xueqi’s palms were suddenly drenched in cold sweat.

In that single instant, the nagging puzzle that had lingered in her heart—the mystery of why Tian Buyi had suddenly appeared so strangely bloated—was finally resolved.

The robe that had seemed uncomfortably tight around Tian Buyi’s unexpectedly bulky frame now slackened. It was glaringly obvious that Tian Buyi had never gained weight; his build was the same as always. The sole reason he had appeared bulkier was that his garments had been drawn taut over something hidden within. And now, the back of his robe had split open, unraveling the horrifying truth before the eyes of Lu Xueqi and Ghost Li.

An ancient, weathered sword hilt jutted quietly from the fabric fluttering in the wind. It sat there peacefully, noiselessly, thrust deeply into Tian Buyi’s back.

Ghost Li’s entire body began to tremble. Even his lips lost all trace of blood, quivering violently. His expression looked as though he wanted to scream at the top of his lungs, yet when the words reached his lips, not a sound emerged.

The world-shaking clash from moments before froze solid. Ghost Li and Lu Xueqi stared blankly at Tian Buyi, as though they had completely forgotten the terrifying nemesis standing behind them. Yet Reverend Daoxuan did not ambush them; he merely watched with a curl of a smile on his lips.

An icy, merciless smile.

With his hand held like a blade, Daoxuan’s five slender fingers suddenly curved. Spontaneous black vapor twirled and dipped between his fingertips before slowly dispersing. In response to this gesture, Tian Buyi let out a muffled groan in the distance. His body convulsed violently as if struck by lightning, his head rearing back toward the sky with an aura of total despair.

*Snap!*

A soft, sickening sound echoed. Under the petrified gazes of Ghost Li and Lu Xueqi, a broken blade of ancient, stone-like substance without a tip pierced through Tian Buyi’s chest, protruding outward.

Strangely, not a single drop of blood flowed from Tian Buyi’s chest.

*Zhuxian!*

That peerless, invincible ancient sword had impaled Tian Buyi’s body through and through!

The black mist on Tian Buyi’s face seemed to come alive with renewed malice, crawling rampantly and engulfing his features. Tian Buyi’s head slowly slumped forward against his chest. His body seemed to lose all vitality; first, his beloved Caihong Sword completely lost its luster, slipping from his lifeless fingers and tumbling toward the earth. Immediately after, that stout figure that had surfaced countless times in Ghost Li’s fondest memories swayed several times before plunging downward from the sky.

Like a shooting star burned to ash, hurtling toward its final resting place—the earth.

Ghost Li shook uncontrollably. He had believed he was strong enough to face any calamity the mortal world could throw at him, yet at this moment, he felt as if he had been cast back ten years. The familiar reek of despair swallowed him whole like a savage, ravenous beast.

"Ahhh—!"

He unleashed a hysterical, soul-tearing howl, diving downward with reckless abandon toward that falling body, toward that familiar figure! His speed was blinding, impossible to check. Sweeping through the gale, a split second before Tian Buyi hit the ground, Ghost Li caught the body of the master who had raised him to manhood.

The touch was icy cold—utterly devoid of life!

This was clearly a corpse that had been dead for days, devoid of even a trace of body heat. Ghost Li held Tian Buyi tightly in his arms, his gasping breaths turning heavier by the second. Without realizing it, tears were streaming down his face.

"Watch out!"

A panicked cry rang out from behind as Lu Xueqi’s white silhouette darted downward. High above in the clouds, Reverend Daoxuan traced a wide circle with his hand, abruptly halting his low, occult incantation.

Ghost Li’s instincts flashed with warning, yet the overwhelming grief and love surging through his soul suppressed all reason.

He did not let go.

This body, this man, had raised him since boyhood, taught him cultivation, and instructed him in the ways of virtue. For decades, he had lived, walked, and forged ahead looking upon this man's back... Every smile, every voice, every memory etched into his soul pounded like thunder in his mind, leaving him frozen.

How could he let go?

The Zhuxian Sword lit up, its sudden radiance stinging his eyes and blinding his vision. That all-too-familiar stench of despair from a decade ago engulfed him once more.

In the nick of time, Lu Xueqi threw everything she had into catching up. Leaping forward, she grabbed Ghost Li and, carried by immense momentum, dragged them both rolling across the ground.

*Rumble!*

Like bursting lightning, the brilliance vanished in an instant. The radiant halo collapsed inward as the ancient Zhuxian Sword tore relentlessly through Tian Buyi's chest, soaring into the heavens beside Reverend Daoxuan. A powerful, slender hand reached out and grasped the hilt. In that breath, heaven and earth plunged into utter gloom; even the scattered stars in the far reaches of the sky were swallowed by storm clouds.

There was no blood. Not a single drop!

Ghost Li looked as if his soul had been torn away, all sensation stripped from him. Struggling to his hands and knees, he crawled frantically toward Tian Buyi. Lu Xueqi reached out to support him, but her hand froze midway.

She threw herself over Ghost Li, grabbing him with ragged breaths born of primal terror: "Look... look at Uncle-Master Tian's hand..."

*Boom!*

A deafening thunderclap rolled across the firmament, and from the dense, suffocating clouds, raindrops began to fall.

The rain was pitch black.

Accompanied by rolling thunder and escalating rain, lightning danced across the sky like serpentine dragons, cleaving the darkness. In the mud, upon Tian Buyi's corpse, one of his fingers twitched.

Ghost Li froze. The agonizing sorrow on his face was instantly replaced by uncontrollable, wild ecstasy! He cried out at the top of his lungs: "Master!"

He lunged forward, charging toward Tian Buyi with wild abandon.

Lu Xueqi’s face was ashen, yet her mind retained more clarity than Ghost Li's. In alarm, she reached out to grab him, but caught only the hem of his robe, which tore with a loud rip.

High in the sky, Reverend Daoxuan stood amidst the dark clouds, enveloped in black mist as the rain and wind parted around him. His face was twisted with monstrous malice, looking like a demon god gazing down with haughty contempt at the mortals below, as though holding their destinies in his palm.

In his hand, the ancient Zhuxian Sword—neither stone nor jade—pulsed with a sinister glow that cast a gruesome pall over his face.

Seeing a glimmer of hope amidst absolute despair, Ghost Li surged forward with ecstatic joy, completely failing to notice that the black mist on Tian Buyi’s face had not dissipated with the loss of vitality; instead, it grew thicker, entirely smothering his features.

Just as Ghost Li rushed over, arms outstretched to embrace his master, Tian Buyi’s palm abruptly whipped upward! Imbued with immense, crushing force, it struck Ghost Li squarely in the chest like a battering ram!

The expression on Ghost Li’s face instantly froze.

A split second later, his body flew backward through the air, bones crackling in rapid succession along the way. Tian Buyi’s cultivation was renowned across the world; the lethality of that unreserved strike was terrifying. Caught completely off-guard, an unknown number of Ghost Li's ribs were shattered, his internal organs violently displaced. It was only thanks to his cultivation of the *Heavenly Tome* and the automatic protection of Tian Yin Temple’s Great Fan Wisdom that he was not killed on the spot. Even so, he was blasted thirty feet away, his vision plunging into blackness with bursts of gold, his chest entirely numb with agony.

Yet in his mind, only one frantic question echoed: *What happened to Master? What happened to Master?!*

"Hahahaha—!"

A blood-chilling laugh cascaded down from the heavens. Standing in the clouds, Reverend Daoxuan laughed maniacally: "Didn't you want to perish together with me? Didn't you want to rid the world of evil? How does it feel to taste the power of this ancient Zhuxian Sword? Hahahaha! Hahahaha—!"

Tian Buyi’s body slowly stood upright. Though his movements seemed stiff, every motion oozed eerie, unnatural power. The black mist on his face churned madly, sending shudders of dread into the soul.

Tian Buyi slowly extended his right hand. Spreading his fingers, the Caihong Sword lying in the distance suddenly flared with light and flew back into his grip. Clasping Caihong, he stepped forward with dragging, measured paces, heading straight toward the severely wounded Ghost Li!

The black rain fell heavier and faster!

*Clang!*

Like a dragon’s cry, the Tianya Sword unsheathed. Lu Xueqi, her face deathly pale, stood defensively before Ghost Li, sword held across her chest. Her breath was ragged, rain lashing against her skin. Her mud-splattered white robes added a tragic, haunting beauty to her figure.

The rain beat against the ground, turning the earth into mud. Blood seeped from the corners of Ghost Li’s mouth, dyeing his robes crimson. His voice was cracked and broken: "Master... you... what has happened to you?"

Tian Buyi seemed deaf to all sound. The howling gale and torrential downpour did not affect him in the slightest. His body simply marched forward toward the struggling Ghost Li and the pale, resolute Lu Xueqi, every step dripping with murderous intent.

*Boom!*

A lightning bolt struck directly overhead, shocking even Reverend Daoxuan in the clouds. An extraordinarily bizarre change manifested across Daoxuan's face.

It was an expression of profound confusion, as though he were suspended between sleep and waking, remembering something yet unable to grasp it, utterly lost.

Corresponding to Daoxuan’s disorientation, the sinister light flickering on the Zhuxian Sword in his hand dimmed.

*Rumble!*

Thunder shook the earth like a titan’s hammer, as though the gods in heaven were roaring in wrath.

In that lightning flash, Tian Buyi’s footsteps suddenly stopped. The dense black mist masking his face receded ever so slightly, revealing his eyes.

How long was a single instant?

Buddhists spoke of a mustard seed holding Mount Sumeru, of an eternity in a fleeting breath. But what kind of moment was this single breath of time?

Those eyes gazed deeply at Ghost Li, watching him struggle in the mud, spitting blood, crying out for his master.

The light of Caihong burned like fire, reflecting in his eyes.

How long could that fleeting instant last?

Tian Buyi jerked his head around with all his remaining will, searching through the gloom until he found Lu Xueqi, staring deeply into her eyes.

Lightning flashed! Thunder crashed!

The wind and rain howled!

The Tianya Celestial Sword radiated soft azure luminescence amidst the storm.

At that moment, Lu Xueqi’s gaze locked with Tian Buyi’s!

Like thunder, like lightning, like a raging storm, she saw the surging, oceanic emotion pouring from those eyes—staring unblinkingly at her with boundless meaning, which ultimately resolved into silence!

In the next breath, every drop of blood drained from Lu Xueqi’s face. Even her lips turned translucent.

Thunder rumbled past. Reverend Daoxuan trembled, his confusion vanishing, and the sinister light on the Zhuxian Sword flared anew.

Down below, the storm continued to howl. Tian Buyi’s eyes were once again drowned beneath the thick, suffocating black mist.

His boots stepped heavily into the mire, splashing muddy water as he advanced toward his target, step by unrelenting step.

Murderous intent chilled the air to bone!

"Uncle... Uncle-Master Tian..." Lu Xueqi’s voice shook with agony, filled with desperate sorrow: "Don't come closer... I beg you, do not come closer..."

Ghost Li propped himself up with his arms, but halfway up, his strength failed him, and he collapsed back into the mud. Sludge splattered across his face, yet he felt nothing.

He could only watch as his resurrected master marched toward them.

Caihong burned fiercely, incinerating the soul.

Through the rain, Tian Buyi drew near. Lu Xueqi’s hand trembled upon Tianya’s hilt, her face white as parchment.

"Uncle-Master Tian... stop! Stop!"

Her answer was the Caihong Sword.

Blazing flames cleaved downward, evaporating every raindrop within three feet. Controlled by that occult power, Tian Buyi’s strength seemed amplified rather than weakened!

Lu Xueqi barely raised Tianya to parry. With a sharp *clang*, she was thrown backward by the colossal force like a kite with a severed string, flying past Ghost Li to land behind Tian Buyi.

Between master and disciple, no obstacle remained.

Tian Buyi halted, slowly raising Caihong. Ghost Li lay helpless upon the ground, his eyes wide, staring at Tian Buyi. Yet Tian Buyi’s face was shrouded in darkness, revealing nothing.

The wind howled; the rain wept.

Suddenly, Tian Buyi bellowed, Caihong erupting with blinding flames as it slashed down toward Ghost Li’s skull! Ghost Li did not dodge—in truth, he had no strength to move. His mouth hung slightly open, perhaps shouting, but all sound was swallowed by the scorching gale of Caihong.

*Boom!*

Lightning pierced the sky, illuminating the desolate earth!

Tian Buyi froze.

Caihong halted a mere foot above Ghost Li’s forehead. Ghost Li could clearly feel the searing heat threatening to burn him to ash.

Yet the blow never landed!

All of Tian Buyi’s motion ceased. The blazing fire of Caihong quietly extinguished. Protruding from Tian Buyi’s chest, directly through his heart, was the tip of a blade.

Glowing with soft azure radiance, wreathed in immortal vapor, the Tianya Celestial Sword had pierced through Tian Buyi’s back, skewering his heart!

The wind and rain lashed against the figure standing behind Tian Buyi. Disheveled hair clung to her face; raindrops streamed down her cheeks. Her face was ashen, her entire body shivering.

*Boom! Boom! Boom!*

Three consecutive thunderbolts detonated around Reverend Daoxuan in the clouds. Daoxuan convulsed in agony, curling inward with a wretched screech. A heartbeat later, he transformed into a streak of black light, fleeing into the horizon.

On the earth below, Ghost Li was paralyzed, staring blankly at the sword tip protruding from his master’s chest.

There was no blood. Not a single drop!

*Clatter!*

Caihong lost all its glow, falling into the mud like scrap metal. Ghost Li’s facial muscles twitched, his body trembling violently.

Then, Tian Buyi’s knees buckled. He sank slowly into the mud before Ghost Li. The black mist on his face began to dissipate rapidly, though a faint layer still clung to his skin.

Lu Xueqi’s hand gripping Tianya trembled, yet she closed her eyes without hesitation. Azure radiance burst from the divine sword, utilizing its millennial righteous aura to purge the demonic corruption from within Tian Buyi’s body. Yet as it cleansed the darkness, it expanded the fatal wound in Tian Buyi’s chest tenfold!

"Urrgh—!"

A beast-like, desperate howl tore from Ghost Li’s throat, tears flooding his cheeks. Summoning impossible strength from his broken body, he lunged forward, throwing his arms around Tian Buyi and pulling him free from Tianya. At that very moment, the last wisp of black mist was purged.

Tian Buyi’s familiar, weary face emerged once more in the rain.

His eyes were open—perhaps they had never closed.

The corners of his mouth twitched, and he offered Ghost Li a faint smile.

Behind him, Lu Xueqi looked as though all vitality had been drained from her soul. Her legs gave way, and she collapsed into the mud.

Ghost Li needed only one glance at the wound to know that the master who had raised him was at the very end of his life, beyond all salvation.

"Why... why?!" he screamed hoarsely, rain and tears blurring together. He turned his glare toward Lu Xueqi, thrashing in the mud as if to crawl over and demand answers.

Yet a trembling hand stopped him.

The hand was weak and fragile, yet Ghost Li was pulled back instantly. Panting, his lips quivering, he rasped, "Master... Master..."

Tian Buyi looked at him, his breath faint as thread. Gathering the last dregs of strength in his broken frame, he struggled to speak:

"Do... do not... blame... her... It is not... her fault!"

Ghost Li grasped Tian Buyi’s hand tightly, feeling only icy coldness.

Unable to endure any longer, he broke down into wailing sobs, weeping bitterly in the stormy night, able only to repeat two words: "Master... Master..."

Tian Buyi gazed at him, his voice growing fainter: "Old Seventh..."

"Master, I am here! I am here!" Ghost Li pressed his face against Tian Buyi's hand, his tears wetting the cold palm.

"After I die... you... bring my body back... to Big Bamboo Peak... give it to... your Shiniang..."

Ghost Li nodded desperately, his face contorted in agony. Under his gaze, Tian Buyi’s breath grew shallower and softer:

"You... you must... console her... tell her not to grieve... do not let her do... anything... foolish... ah..."

With that final cry, Tian Buyi’s voice spiked, and then abruptly ceased.

The hand held within Ghost Li’s grip fell limp.

Ghost Li froze. His shivering body stilled, paralyzed in place.

The bleak, freezing storm pierced straight into the depths of his soul.

After an eternity, he whispered brokenly: "Master..."

Then, his vision went black, and he collapsed unconscious beside Tian Buyi’s body.
