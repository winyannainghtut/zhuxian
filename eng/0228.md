# Chapter 228 - Punishing Heart

"Tsk, tsk..." A rasping, eerie laugh came from the mysterious figure, jarring everyone back to reality. "A reunion between master and disciple—how truly touching!"

Ghost Li exhaled a long breath. Though excitement, joy, shock, and myriad questions flooded his chest, he was no longer that ignorant boy of the past. He quickly composed himself, yet he still could not resist looking at Tian Buyi, his heart swirling with mixed emotions.

Tian Buyi had similarly experienced a tumult of feelings after his initial shock. Yet his life experience was far greater than Ghost Li's. Steeling his mind, his countenance turned stern. He turned to the figure cloaked in black mist and sneered, "Both of them are disciples of my Qing Yun Sect. Since you dared commit these acts, do you lack the courage to face them?"

Ghost Li was taken aback. His gaze first landed on the mysterious figure; he had not expected this person to have ties with the Qing Yun Sect. But a moment later, he looked at Tian Buyi with profound emotion, a complex expression surfacing on his face.

Standing to the side, Lu Xueqi’s lips curved into a faint smile as she looked at Ghost Li. Sensing her gaze, Ghost Li turned to look at her. Lu Xueqi smiled tenderly, a warmth in her eyes that offered both comfort and joy.

Sure enough, the mysterious figure chuckled coldly, pointing at Ghost Li. "What, does he still count as a disciple of Qing Yun?"

Tian Buyi said resolutely, "He is the seventh disciple under my Big Bamboo Peak! I, Tian Buyi, say so! What of it, Senior Brother Daoxuan?"

Ghost Li’s body shook violently, his expression morphing into utter disbelief. After a long pause, his gaze shifted from Tian Buyi to that mysterious figure, asking incredulously, "Reverend Daoxuan?"

The mysterious figure burst into laughter. The black mist enveloping his body churned, gradually dissipating downward, slowly revealing the upper half of his torso. The man had a long beard stroking his chest and an ancient, solemn countenance. If not the revered, world-renowned Sect Master of Qing Yun, who else could it be?

In this fleeting span of time, Ghost Li had endured one shock after another. Seeing Reverend Daoxuan's face, he could not help but take a step back, his mind a tangled web of blankness. And though Lu Xueqi had been mentally prepared, seeing the face of the master she had venerated like a god for decades still caused her heart to quiver, her face turning pale.

Reverend Daoxuan's appearance had not changed, yet one could clearly see that his demeanor was completely different from the past. A cold gleam flickered in his eyes, devoid of any benevolence or mercy.

Ghost Li scrutinized Reverend Daoxuan, his brows slowly knitting together as a sharp glint flashed in his eyes. The person before him was the pivotal figure who had changed the course of his entire life. Ten years ago, atop Tongtian Peak of Mount Qing Yun, that world-shattering strike of the Zhuxian Sword had shattered Biyao’s soul and transformed the boy Zhang Xiaofan into the Ghost Li of today.

Yet Ghost Li understood deeply how terrifying Reverend Daoxuan’s cultivation was. Entering the Ghost King Sect to study the Heavenly Tome, he had long known that if he merely cultivated Qing Yun's Taoist methods and Tian Yin Temple's Buddhist arts, he might spend his entire life without ever matching the Zhuxian Sword Formation. The harder he trained, the deeper his understanding of mystical arts became, yet his hopes grew bleaker—especially ten years later during the Beast Calamity, when Reverend Daoxuan had unleashed the Heavenly Mechanism Lock upon Mount Qing Yun, drawing upon heaven and earth's spiritual energy to crush the invincible Beast Deity. That godlike majesty was far beyond mortal reach.

Yet he had never dreamed he would encounter a Reverend Daoxuan radiating such horrific demonic energy. Though his cultivation remained unfathomably deep, to Ghost Li, he finally seemed within reach.

A trace of agitation flickered across Ghost Li's face.

Reverend Daoxuan suddenly turned, staring at Ghost Li with cold light in his eyes.

Having governed Qing Yun for over a century, his fame spanned the four seas. When Ghost Li was still Zhang Xiaofan, he had regarded Reverend Daoxuan as a living immortal. Being stared at so abruptly, an inexplicable tension rose within Ghost Li, and he subconsciously clenched his fists.

"You wish to kill me?" Reverend Daoxuan asked chillingly.

Ghost Li did not speak. He simply flicked his wrist. A green light flashed, and the Soul-Devouring Stick appeared in his palm, its gentle jade radiance carrying streaks of eerie crimson flowing through the dark iron.

For some reason, Reverend Daoxuan’s gaze swept across the Soul-Devouring Stick, a sneer curling at his lips. He turned to Tian Buyi and asked the exact same question: "Do you wish to kill me?"

Tian Buyi replied with utmost bluntness, "Yes."

"Can you kill me?"

Tian Buyi snorted. "Even if I cannot, I must still strike!"

Reverend Daoxuan laughed sharply, the piercing sound carrying far into the night. "Indeed. I saw it long ago—aside from myself and that dead ghost Wan Jianyi, you are truly the foremost among our generation in Qing Yun in both talent and courage."

A shade of sorrow crossed Tian Buyi’s face. "What is the use of speaking of such things now?"

Reverend Daoxuan’s brows shot up as he bellowed, "Fine! Then let me ask you: why do you wish to kill me?"

Tian Buyi shook, momentarily at a loss for words.

For some reason, an expression of profound bitterness appeared on Reverend Daoxuan's face, completely at odds with his sinister aura. He said coldly, "Junior Brother Tian, you wish to kill me—is it for the sake of the righteousness, morality, and justice you hold so dear?"

This was the first time in many days Tian Buyi had heard him utter the words "Junior Brother Tian." A wave of confusion stirred in his chest, but he gritted his teeth and countered, "Since falling to demons, you have committed atrocities without end. If I do not slay you, I fear the sins you accrue will only grow vaster!"

Reverend Daoxuan threw his head back and howled, his voice laced with boundless mockery. He glared at Tian Buyi. "What a righteous and stern declaration! Then let me ask you: do you remember *why* I became like this today?"

Tian Buyi was stunned, left entirely speechless.

Reverend Daoxuan snorted, glancing toward Lu Xueqi, who stood on high guard. "Your uncle-master refuses to speak. Can you tell me?"

Lu Xueqi's face paled further. She subconsciously averted her eyes, falling silent.

Indeed, what could be said? Could one say that Reverend Daoxuan had risked his life twice to activate the Zhuxian Sword Formation—ten years ago for the righteous path, and ten years later for the common people—only to end up like this?

Cause and effect, right and wrong, good and evil—all were so hopelessly entangled! Fate made fools of mortals to such an extent!

Inside the ruined morgue, the initiative was entirely seized by Reverend Daoxuan. Tian Buyi had originally been prepared to sacrifice his life for justice, never expecting that though Reverend Daoxuan was corrupt, his supernatural cultivation remained intact, and his mind was astonishingly lucid. With just a few questions, Tian Buyi was left trembling, unable to raise his blade.

These truths were crystal clear not only to Tian Buyi, but to Lu Xueqi as well. If weighed fairly, Reverend Daoxuan had performed meritorious deeds bordering on salvation for the world. In normal times, erecting shrines and carving monuments for him would hardly be excessive. Yet fate had cruelly brought him to this end. Had he been completely maddened into mindless slaughter, Tian Buyi and the others would have fought without hesitation. But these pointed questions struck them right in their hearts.

How should the principles of the world be reconciled?

At that moment, Ghost Li spoke coldly: "The common people of this world have nothing to do with me. Whatever merit you hold, I care not. I only demand the debt of that one sword strike ten years ago that doomed Biyao!"

Before his voice faded, the Soul-Devouring Stick flared with dazzling jade luminescence! A chilling aura surged from all directions, instantly flooding every corner of the morgue. A sharp whistle tore through the air as Ghost Li drove his treasure straight down the center!

Reverend Daoxuan’s expression shifted slightly. He had clearly not expected Ghost Li's cultivation to have advanced so dramatically over ten years. The Soul-Devouring Stick seemed slow yet arrived like lightning, cloaked in thunder and gale. The emerald radiance shimmering on it was the quintessential Taiji Xuanqing Dao of Qing Yun, and its realm had undeniably broken through the Jade Pure Realm to attain the Shang Pure Realm. In Reverend Daoxuan's expert eyes, the youth had reached the pinnacle of the Shang Pure Realm, faintly bordering on the legendary Tai Pure Realm!

Tian Buyi and Lu Xueqi were both stellar figures of Qing Yun; witnessing this blow, both changed color and exchanged a glance of profound shock.

Tian Buyi’s eyes held pure wonder, while in Lu Xueqi’s eyes, past the astonishment, was a faint trace of heartache that was quickly overwhelmed by genuine joy.

Naturally, none of the three knew that Ghost Li had endured immense tribulations over the years, blessed by bizarre coincidences. He was the sole person in the world to have comprehended four volumes of the supreme divine scripture—the *Heavenly Tome*. The origins of the Heavenly Tome were ancient and mysterious, its techniques infinitely varied. Most of the world's greatest sects derived their arts from it: Tian Yin Temple's methods stemmed from the fourth volume, and the Demonic Sect's various arts flowed from it as well. With Ghost Li practicing four volumes simultaneously, his cultivation was terrifying.

Yet though Ghost Li was extraordinary, Reverend Daoxuan was a peerless titan who had mastered the Taiji Xuanqing Dao to the Tai Pure Realm to wield the Zhuxian Sword Formation. At this moment, everyone truly witnessed his boundless power.

Before the Soul-Devouring Stick even arrived, the black mist around Reverend Daoxuan churned furiously, pressed back by the crushing force. Yet after his initial surprise, Reverend Daoxuan remained steady as a mountain. His right hand emerged from the mist, his slender fingers forming a sword-incantation and drawing swift arcs through the empty air. Green light flared from his fingertips, instantly tracing a brilliant Taiji Yin-Yang diagram!

Once formed, the diagram rotated endlessly. Gale-force winds whipped around it, illuminating the darkness. Within breaths, it became a rapidly spinning vortex of celestial energy.

The Soul-Devouring Stick slammed into the Taiji diagram! Both forces carried titanic weight, yet at the moment of impact, save for a tiny whistle, not a single sound rang out.

The Taiji diagram was forcefully pushed back three feet by the stick. Yet those three feet exhausted all of the Soul-Devouring Stick’s momentum, halting its advance. Its emerald glow dimmed as it began to drift with the vortex.

Ghost Li roared, leaping into midair as the stick looped backward. Reverend Daoxuan sneered, flicking his fingers to send the blazing Taiji diagram soaring straight toward the figure in the air!

Piercing whistles echoed across the sky as Ghost Li’s form flickered like a phantom, shifting with incredible speed. Yet Reverend Daoxuan possessed divine sight, completely unfooled by the illusions. The Taiji diagram pursued Ghost Li relentlessly, closing the distance.

Finally, Ghost Li’s speed slowed, and the diagram caught up. Cold light flashed in Daoxuan's eyes as the Taiji diagram expanded fourfold, crashing downward!

Lu Xueqi’s body quivered, while Tian Buyi slowly nodded in contemplation.

As the diagram struck, Daoxuan’s face suddenly changed. With a bizarre hiss, he abandoned the diagram and shot straight into the clouds! The diagram hit empty air as Ghost Li vanished, and the formation dispersed in the wind.

The moment Daoxuan leaped into the sky, a thunderous roar erupted from beneath the morgue courtyard! The ground within twenty feet trembled violently. Moments later, like a raging earth dragon bursting into the world, all the soil and stones blasted upward with a deafening crash! The ruined chamber collapsed into splinters, mixing with dirt and rock. All of it was drawn by an invisible celestial force into a massive torrent of stone and earth, roaring toward Reverend Daoxuan in midair!

In an instant, the roles of offense and defense had dramatically reversed!

Yet suspended in midair, Reverend Daoxuan showed no fear. Watching the titanic earthen torrent hurtling toward him, the black mist around him expanded, coalescing into a two-foot square black barrier before him.

*Boom!*

The earthen torrent smashed heavily into the black barrier! Like a thunderbolt from the heavens, faint sparks of lightning skittered across the point of impact. A colossal shockwave swept across heaven and earth, radiating outward like a tidal wave centered on the black barrier.

The derelict morgue was utterly obliterated. Everything was ground to powder and blown into the distance. Even the lonely trees nearby were torn out by their roots and sent tumbling away.

Tian Buyi and Lu Xueqi had long since taken to the air, avoiding the rolling sea of mud, yet the gale-force winds in the sky cut against them like blades, their cultivation barely shielding them from the sting.

Yet in the center of the clash, the tide turned once more. The seemingly thin black barrier slowly pressed downward through the thunder and lightning, gradually suppressing the massive earthen dragon.

Though Ghost Li’s cultivation had advanced by leaps and bounds, he was ultimately no match for Reverend Daoxuan’s centuries of meditation.

Watching the thunderous battlefield, Tian Buyi’s eyes churned with resolve. After a moment of hesitation, he gritted his teeth, the Caihong Sword blazing with scarlet light. With a roar, he merged with his blade and shot into the fray!

Behind him, Lu Xueqi gasped softly.

The Caihong Sword burned like a raging inferno, wrapping Tian Buyi in resplendent flames like a soaring phoenix. Though his round stature made the posture slightly awkward, his cultivation was formidable. Reverend Daoxuan dared not take it lightly, swiveling midair to dodge the brunt of the strike.

The black barrier dispersed, returning to envelop Daoxuan. The massive earthen torrent collapsed, raining dirt from the sky. Out from the dust emerged Ghost Li, his expression solemn but showing no sign of breathlessness.

In that single clash, everyone saw that Daoxuan’s mastery was godlike. Though Ghost Li had comprehended four volumes of the Heavenly Tome, his years of cultivation were still shorter than Daoxuan's Tai Pure realm, yet he was strong enough to contend with him.

Tian Buyi’s blade slashed empty air, but the crimson flames only blazed brighter. Sweeping across the sky like a rainbow, he curved back to strike Daoxuan again.

Daoxuan dodged once more, sneering, "What, you can act now? Have you sorted out your righteousness and morality?"

Tian Buyi snorted, holding his blade across his chest. "Your merit is immense, yet your sins are grave! My strike is for the command of the past patriarchs! If you die by my hand today, I shall slit my throat to atone for it!"

Ghost Li shook slightly, glancing at Tian Buyi in confusion. But before anyone could explain, Tian Buyi roared again, his flames blanketing the heavens. Ghost Li set aside his doubts and flew forward to attack as well.

Besieged by two supreme experts, Reverend Daoxuan’s peerless cultivation was displayed in full. His finger-incantations danced, while his dense Xuan Yin Ghost Qi transformed endlessly between offense and defense. Fighting one against two in midair, he held off both Tian Buyi and Ghost Li without yielding an inch!

Amidst the fierce clash, Tian Buyi cleaved downward with mountain-splitting force, forcing Daoxuan to retreat three feet. Seizing that fleeting breath, Tian Buyi shouted at the top of his lungs:

"Old Seventh, be careful! He still has the Zhuxian Sword on him!"

Ghost Li’s heart jolted. The terror of the ancient Zhuxian Sword was etched into his very soul. Yet a heartbeat later, the name *Zhuxian* felt like boiling oil poured over his heart, setting his entire bloodline ablaze.

Ten years ago, atop Mount Qing Yun, that falling green figure!

Instantly, Ghost Li’s eyes turned blood-red. With a savage roar, he threw himself forward, every technique unleashed with suicidal abandon! Tian Buyi was shocked, then sighed inwardly and charged in to support him.

Ghost Li's sudden frenzy caught Reverend Daoxuan off-guard, putting him on the back foot for several exchanges. Yet his cultivation was transcendent; weathering Ghost Li’s suicidal strikes and Tian Buyi's relentless fire, he stubbornly clawed back the initiative, revealing no signs of collapse.

Tian Buyi grew increasingly alarmed. He had known his senior brother was an unearthly genius, but according to ancestral lore, anyone corrupted by the Zhuxian Sword should suffer a drastic drop in cultivation. Yet Daoxuan's strength remained as terrifying as ever. Could it be that his cultivation was so high that the sword could corrupt his mind, but could not erode his power?

As Ghost Li's attacks grew more frantic, Daoxuan's defense held like iron, his sporadic counterattacks lethal and vicious. Were it not for Tian Buyi's desperate saves, Ghost Li would have already been gravely wounded.

Seeing the mortal danger repeated, Tian Buyi was fraught with anxiety when a clear, celestial whistle rang out. A white-clad figure descended like an immortal maiden, the Tianya Sword radiating divine azure light!

Lu Xueqi finally joined the battle.

With three supreme experts united, the balance shattered! No matter how formidable Daoxuan was, he could not withstand the combined might of three masters wielding divine artifacts while fighting empty-handed. Step by step, the crushing pressure mounted upon him.

Yet a lingering doubt brushed through the minds of the three: where was the all-powerful Zhuxian Sword? Why had Daoxuan not drawn it?

Just as the three were about to overpower him, a cold glint flashed in Daoxuan's eyes as he swept his gaze across them.

The trio shuddered under his gaze. Before they could react, a tremendous shockwave erupted from Daoxuan's hands, forcing them to brace their defenses. Seizing that split second, a dark light flashed across Daoxuan's face. Cupping his left hand and raising his right in a blade-gesture, he rapidly chanted an occult incantation. Black mist flared on his fingertips, flashing three times before dispersing into the void.

The night sky turned wretched and weeping!

A sound seemed to howl in the heavens, surging with boundless Yin energy and storming clouds.

Ghost Li and Lu Xueqi recognized that Daoxuan was unleashing a lethal curse and surged forward to interrupt him. Yet just as they moved, an extraordinarily bizarre sound—like something crisp and fragile suddenly snapping apart—rang out directly from beside them!

A shiver of sheer terror shot through their souls like lightning.

Both froze in midair and turned slowly.

Behind them, a short, stout silhouette had halted even earlier.

Tian Buyi’s face retained the expression from the previous moment, yet it appeared completely rigid. He remained suspended in the air, the crimson flame of Caihong still glowing, but his body was completely motionless.

That horrifying, soul-rending snap had come unmistakably from inside Tian Buyi’s own body!
