# Chapter 225 - Rescuing

Inside the derelict morgue, with Lu Xueqi's sudden appearance, the atmosphere abruptly turned rather peculiar.

Zhou Yixian furrowed his brows and forced a dry laugh, saying, "Isn't this Heroine Lu from Mount Qing Yun? How have you also ended up in a place like this?"

Lu Xueqi glanced at Zhou Yixian, and then her gaze naturally fell upon Xiao Bai. Xiao Bai smiled faintly, her captivating eyes rippling with light as she measured Lu Xueqi in return.

Lu Xueqi's delicate brows knitted slightly. She turned away and asked Xiao Huan, "Lady Xiao Huan, was the magical treasure you mentioned earlier truly wielded by the person who saved you?"

Xiao Huan nodded firmly. "Indeed, that was exactly what it looked like. I remember it very clearly."

A flicker of uncertainty crossed Lu Xueqi’s face as if she were lost in thought. Without lingering, she continued, "Where is that person now? And where is the other... devil you mentioned earlier? Do you know his identity?"

Xiao Bai, standing to the side, also took on an attentive expression. 

Without needing to think, Xiao Huan answered directly, "I could not make out the devil’s origin or identity. I only know that his cultivation is unfathomably deep. After capturing us, he locked us in those filthy coffins and then vanished. Usually, he only shows up once every three to five days. I remember he came back yesterday, and after that, he wasn't seen again. He likely won't return for several more days!"

Lu Xueqi murmured an "Oh," her brow furrowing even tighter. "And the one who saved you?"

Xiao Huan pointed behind her. "Isn't he right there in the coffin in the corner of that inner room?"

Lu Xueqi was startled. Xiao Bai, standing beside her, also changed color slightly. With Xiao Bai's immense cultivation, she had surprisingly failed to detect that someone else was still inside that corner coffin earlier.

Lu Xueqi nodded. After a moment of contemplation, she walked toward that pitch-black, dilapidated room. Watching her back, Xiao Huan could not help calling out, "Be careful!"

Lu Xueqi's steps paused. She turned back to look at Xiao Huan, smiled faintly, and nodded. Gathering her spirits, she stepped onto the moss-covered stone steps.

The stone steps numbered only three or four, and she crossed them in a few paces. The darkness within the small room was as thick as ever, allowing only vague silhouettes to be discerned. However, besides the faint starlight filtering in from the doorway, a large hole had just been smashed into the dilapidated wall, making the room slightly brighter than when Xiao Bai had first entered.

Lu Xueqi quickly spotted the coffin lying in the corner. That spot was the darkest recess of the chamber, furthest from any light. Faintly, the Yin energy felt strongest there—which was precisely why the Shaman Demon had instinctively made a beeline for it upon entering the building.

Lu Xueqi drew a deep breath. At this moment, her cultivation had already reached perfection in her own Qing Yun Sect's Taoist methods. Moreover, blessed with peerless intelligence and natural talent, the third volume of the Heavenly Tome she had jointly committed to memory with Ghost Li in the Western Death Marsh had aided her immensely during her private cultivation. She had always been exceedingly discreet, so very few people could fathom her true cultivation level today.

Yet standing inside this derelict morgue chamber, Lu Xueqi almost subconsciously frowned. The sheer density of the Yin energy here far exceeded her expectations. When she had stood just paces away outside, she had not sensed it in the slightest.

Evidently, an expert of supreme skill had laid down an occult restriction, forcefully locking that virulent, gloomy energy within this confined space.

This level of cultivation alone was extraordinary! Furthermore, such intense Yin energy could never have arisen from an ordinary morgue. Who could have set up such a bizarre esoteric restriction, and who was the person trapped within it? Could it truly be as she suspected?

Unconsciously, Lu Xueqi realized her palms were slick with cold sweat. Yet she was no ordinary mortal; her will was steadfast. Though doubt churned within her, there was no hint of cowardice. Circulating her divine spiritual energy, she remained on high alert and walked over, step by measured step.

Inside the coffin, the Shaman Demon and the mysterious stout man beneath him watched the white silhouette approach. The stout man showed no reaction, merely watching, while the Shaman Demon’s mind was in total turmoil, unsure of what Lu Xueqi intended to do once she drew near. He desperately wished to escape this awkward situation, but during this time, no matter how many tricks or cunning arts he had attempted, the bizarre restriction in this coffin seemed uniquely tailored to counter him. It locked his meridians so tightly that he could not budge an inch, unable to summon even a shred of strength.

The Shaman Demon lamented inwardly, feeling completely helpless and endlessly cursing his rotten luck.

Lu Xueqi steadily approached the mysterious coffin. The closer she drew to the seemingly plain wooden casket, the more pronounced the vigilance and faint shock between her brows became. This coffin was obviously no peerless treasure; judging by its material, it was at best mediocre timber, and more than half of it was rotten. It certainly was not the coffin itself radiating such overwhelming Yin energy.

With her acute perception at such close range, she confirmed that two people were indeed inside. Surrounding them was an ethereal, nebulous barrier of dark, malicious energy enveloping their bodies. Even though this layer of Yin energy was three feet away, Lu Xueqi felt her own blood and Qi faintly roiling, sending chills pulsing against her.

What kind of sorcery, or what unheard-of demonic artifact, possessed such inconceivable power?

Lu Xueqi suppressed the astonishment in her heart, settled her mind, and subdued the faint restlessness in her vitality. She scrutinized the coffin carefully, then slowly reached out her hand toward it.

At the entrance of the small room, Xiao Bai's figure appeared. Leaning against the doorframe, she looked relaxed, yet her beautiful eyes were fixed intently on Lu Xueqi’s every move. With her supreme cultivation, she had already noticed the peculiarity of that corner coffin when rescuing Zhou Yixian's group earlier. The spell binding Zhou Yixian and the others had been utterly commonplace, but the coffin in the corner harbored immense danger. Even she had not dared act rashly. In that split second, she had prioritized freeing Zhou Yixian’s trio while holding back from touching the coffin.

Now watching Lu Xueqi stand before it, Xiao Bai naturally watched with rapt attention. Moreover, the Shaman Demon trapped inside was precisely the person she sought, so she focused all her senses.

On the other side, Zhou Yixian, Xiao Huan, and the Wild Dog Taoist could not resist their curiosity either, quietly edging outside the gaping hole in the wall to peer into the room.

Inside and outside the morgue, an abrupt silence fell. The atmosphere grew eerie. Everyone held their breath, watching Lu Xueqi without daring to blink. Far across the distant night sky, a faint plume of black energy surged and twirled, pursued relentlessly by another soaring silhouette intertwined in midair, yet not a single soul on the ground noticed.

*Snap!*

In the darkness, along with the gentle sway of that white-clad figure, Lu Xueqi’s slender, pale hand slowly brushed against the coffin’s wooden plank. Almost simultaneously, from what should have been a noiseless motion, a crisp, sharp cracking sound rang out from deep inside the casket.

It sounded as if a piece of wood had split apart.

Lu Xueqi’s expression shifted drastically. She retracted her hand with blinding speed. Just as her fingers pulled away, a burst of black mist erupted from the point of contact. A continuous *hissing* noise filled the air as it burned like ghostly fire across that small space. There were no visible flames, yet it burned an exact handprint into the wood matching the size of Lu Xueqi's palm.

Trapped within the coffin, the Shaman Demon felt a chill run through him. The demonic art he practiced bore some resemblance to this binding power, though its potency was leagues apart. Seeing that colorless dark fire flare and extinguish in an instant, his heart quivered. He could well imagine the consequences of being touched by that black fire. Yet he was utterly bewildered: why had this savage restriction not activated when he dove into the coffin earlier, but reacted so ferociously the moment Lu Xueqi touched it?

As he racked his brains in confusion, he suddenly felt a shudder below him. On the mysterious stout man underneath, a bizarre metamorphosis seemed to unfold in tandem with the activation of the restriction.

A wave of concentrated, malicious Yin energy surged forth—hundreds of times more potent than the faint breath from before. It instantly filled the entire interior of the coffin. A deafening roar buzzed in the Shaman Demon’s head as his mind went blank. Countless icy, venomous tendrils drilled into his flesh like vipers, clamping fiercely onto his meridians. The agony was excruciating, yet he could not even utter a whimper. For that fleeting moment, he genuinely felt that death would be a mercy.

And the source of this terrifying Yin energy was unmistakably emanating from within the body of that mysterious stout man!

Lu Xueqi stared at the black handprint, her face pale. Xiao Bai, standing behind her at a distance, slowly straightened up, no longer leaning against the doorframe, her countenance turning solemn.

Moments later, Lu Xueqi seemed to sense something profound. Her body trembled, and her expression changed dramatically, yet she did not retreat or dodge. Without a second’s hesitation, she flicked her wrist. 

*Clang!*

A sound like a celestial dragon’s roar resonated! A faint rosy glow ascended, followed by brilliant azure light radiating in all directions, instantly illuminating the dark, gloomy room.

The Tianya Celestial Sword was unsheathed!

Amidst the billowing auspicious vapor, the autumn-water blade reflected Lu Xueqi’s frost-pure countenance. The sword’s radiance mirrored clear water, rippling in midair like gentle autumn waves on a pond. After a subtle pause, countless phantom sword beams materialized in the air, slashing downward toward the coffin.

Though called a slash, it lacked the brute force that shattered stone. As the sword beam drew near, something inside the mysterious coffin seemed to perceive the lethal threat, and crackling sounds echoed outward.

A pillar of black mist suddenly erupted from the coffin, almost solid in form, catching the Tianya Sword in midair! Lu Xueqi’s expression tightened. With a clear cry, her body soared aloft, her white robes fluttering like an immortal maiden descending from the heavens.

At the spot where she had stood just three feet away, a ferocious *hissing* erupted. That entire circular patch of ground was scorched beyond recognition by a sudden wave of black vapor.

A pungent, charred stench permeated the room.

Midair, Lu Xueqi remained unruffled. The Tianya Sword shone resplendently as she carved a graceful arc and thrust downward once more.

By now, rolling clouds of sinister black mist completely enveloped the coffin. It was impossible to tell how so much murky vapor had gathered in mere seconds. Azure lightning flashed above as Tianya descended with dazzling splendor, yet the black mist below did not yield. As though guided by an invisible hand, it coalesced from all four corners toward the center of the coffin, forming a black bulwark against Tianya.

Just as the divine blade was about to collide with the dark barrier, the tip of Tianya touched the black mist and rebounded like a compressed spring. Lu Xueqi’s body drifted backward like a weightless feather. Yet as she soared, her left hand formed an ethereal sword-blade gesture. In an instant, a subtle layer of golden light flickered in her refined eyes, disappearing in a flash, while a jade-green luminescence flared from her palm. It was the purest Taiji Xuanqing Dao of the Qing Yun Sect, cleaving straight into the thinnest seam on the flank of the black mist!

*Bang!*

The black mist violently churned. Following several muffled thuds, something shattered into pieces within.

Xiao Bai, standing at the doorway, curled her lips into a faint smile and nodded with unmistakable approval. On the other side, behind Xiao Huan and the Wild Dog Taoist, Zhou Yixian’s brows furrowed deeply. It seemed he had discerned something profound, his eyes filled with shock and suspicion before plunging into deep contemplation.

Having suffered Lu Xueqi’s unexpected strike, the black mist seemed enraged beyond measure. Switching instantly from defense to attack, it surged upward like a dark storm cloud, hurtling toward the white silhouette in midair.

Lu Xueqi paused in midair, the fierce wind whipping her dark hair across her cheeks. Without hesitation, she allowed an invisible celestial force to elevate her further. With a resounding *boom*, an incredible scene unfolded: the entire dilapidated roof of the morgue shattered into flying debris! Rotten planks and powdery dust rained down like a storm, yet that white-clad figure soared into the sky like a drifting cloud, appearing peerlessly graceful beneath the tranquil starlight.

The black mist pursued her for ten feet before losing momentum. The cool wind sweeping across the open wilderness soon dispersed the dark vapor into nothingness.

Lu Xueqi paused high above, uttered a clear whistle, and dove back down into the ruined room.

Xiao Bai had already leapt out from the doorway, standing with her hands clasped behind her back to watch from afar. Zhou Yixian and the other two were far more disheveled, scrambling desperately to avoid the falling rotten debris.

Amidst the chaos, Lu Xueqi darted back into the room. Her crisp, authoritative shouts rang out, followed by a brilliant burst of blue light shooting through the cracks in countless beams. A moment later, a resounding crash echoed from within, accompanied by a joyous cry from Lu Xueqi.

"Uncle-Master Tian, it truly is you!"

Zhou Yixian and the others stood far away, ensuring they would not be struck by falling junk before turning back. Inside the chaotic room, the turmoil gradually settled. After a short while, the dazzling azure glow subsided. Then, a figure walked out from the doorway.

It was not Lu Xueqi, but a mysterious figure clad entirely in black, his face completely masked. Zhou Yixian and the others did not recognize him, but Xiao Bai gave a soft snort. Without seeming to move, her figure flickered and appeared directly in front of the Shaman Demon, blocking his path.

The Shaman Demon glanced at Xiao Bai, let out a bitter laugh, and halted his steps.

Moments later, footsteps sounded inside the ruined chamber. This time, two people emerged at the entrance: Lu Xueqi was carefully supporting a weary, haggard stout man, slowly walking out.

Xiao Huan and the others looked closely. This stout man was indeed the senior who had saved their lives from that devil days ago! Yet looking at him now, after being confined in that coffin for so long, his stature seemed to have grown noticeably bulkier and stouter.

Lu Xueqi supported the stout man, seating him gently on the stone steps before the entrance, asking softly, "Uncle-Master Tian, are you alright?"

The onlookers were all startled upon hearing her address him. They naturally knew Lu Xueqi’s status, and hearing her call this stout man "Uncle-Master," could this person also be from the Qing Yun Sect—and an elder of senior standing at that?

This stout man was none other than the Head of Big Bamboo Peak, Tian Buyi, who had mysteriously vanished alongside Sect Master Reverend Daoxuan some time ago!

Tian Buyi looked at Lu Xueqi and nodded, saying little. Clever as she was, Lu Xueqi understood and refrained from probing further. Yet for some reason, her heart pounded erratically, and the tension she had felt earlier grew even more intense.

Tian Buyi was here... then where was that other, far more critical person?

Could he truly be the "devil" Xiao Huan and the others had spoken of?

That thought flashed through Lu Xueqi's mind, sending a prickly sensation across her back like needlepoints.

The Shaman Demon stood to the side, his gaze resting deeply on Tian Buyi. This was the first time he had clearly seen Tian Buyi's face, and a strange light flickered in his eyes.

Yet he had no time to ponder others, for Xiao Bai’s voice soon echoed by his ear: "Where is what I want?"

The Shaman Demon’s heart skipped a beat. Forcing another bitter smile, he turned to Xiao Bai and said, "I already told you."

Xiao Bai spat disdainfully. "The southern border is thousands of miles away! Do you expect me to travel all that way on your unverified word? I advise you to honestly hand over what I desire."

The Shaman Demon remained silent for a moment. Hidden behind his mask, his expression was unreadable, but he was clearly contemplating.

Xiao Bai grew impatient. "You know very well who I am. I am not like that old ghost from Fenxiang Valley. Back when your master was alive, he also promised to give me that item!"

The Shaman Demon nodded quietly. Xiao Bai's words seemed to have swayed him. He stepped closer to Xiao Bai and whispered several sentences in a low voice.

Xiao Bai's brows furrowed. "Truly?"

The Shaman Demon said calmly, "This is not your first encounter with shamanic sorcery. You are well aware of the taboos of the ancient Southern Shaman clan."

Xiao Bai pondered for a moment, then nodded. "Fine, I will believe you this once. If you dare deceive me, I will hunt you down sooner or later and make you pay dearly."

The Shaman Demon laughed bitterly and shook his head.

Xiao Bai glanced at the others, her gaze finally settling on Lu Xueqi. Lu Xueqi met her gaze. Xiao Bai smiled bewitchingly, an indescribable charm playing at her lips and brows that bore no vulgarity, only accentuating her peerless beauty. She smiled and said, "Lady Lu, it has been a long time."

Lu Xueqi remained expressionless, merely nodding faintly.

The smile on Xiao Bai's lips deepened. "I wonder, have you seen him recently?"

Lu Xueqi's elegant brows furrowed, and she gave a cold snort as her expression turned frosty. Seeing her reaction, Xiao Bai covered her mouth with a light chuckle, shook her head, turned, and strode away, quickly vanishing into the night.
